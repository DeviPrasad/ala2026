Assume $\MVec{x}$ is not a constant vector, so $\Std{x} \neq 0$.
Therefore, the vector $\MVec{z}$, the
\emph{standardized} version of $\MVec{x}$, is well defined.

\begin{align*}
    \MVec{z} &= \dfrac{\DemeanedVec{x}}{\Std{x}} \\[4pt]
             &= \dfrac{\sqrt{n}}{\Norm{\DemeanedVec{x}}} \, \DemeanedVec{x}
                \qquad\qquad \because \, \Std{x} = \dfrac{\Norm{\DemeanedVec{x}}}{\sqrt{n}}
\end{align*}

Since $\MVec{z}$ is a positive scalar multiple of $\DemeanedVec{x}$,
and scaling preserves the average.

\vspace*{6pt}\noindent{\color{black}{\rule{\textwidth}{1pt}}}
It is easy to see why scaling preserves the average:
\begin{align*}
    \MVec{x} &= (x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n) \\[4pt]
    \Avg{x} &= \dfrac{x_1 + x_2 + \cdots + x_n}{n} \\[4pt]
    \\
    \alpha \MVec{x} &= (\alpha x_1, \alpha x_2, \ldots, \alpha x_n) \\[4pt]
    \mathbf{avg}(\alpha \MVec{x})  &= \dfrac{\alpha x_1 + \alpha x_2 + \cdots + \alpha x_n}{n} \\[4pt]
                    &= \dfrac{\alpha \, (x_1 + x_2 + \cdots + x_n)}{n} \\[4pt]
                    &= \alpha\,\Avg{x}\\[-2em]
\end{align*}
\noindent{\color{black}{\rule{\textwidth}{1pt}}}\\

Therefore,
\begin{align*}
    \Avg{z} &= \mathbf{avg} \left(\dfrac{\sqrt{n}}{\Norm{\DemeanedVec{x}}} \ \ \DemeanedVec{x}\right) \\[4pt]
            &=\left(\,\dfrac{\sqrt{n}}{\Norm{\DemeanedVec{x}}}\,\right) \, \AvgDemeanedVec{x} \\[4pt]
            &= 0 \qquad\qquad \because \, \AvgDemeanedVec{x} = 0
\end{align*}

Let us try to determine $\Std{z}$.

\begin{align*}
    \Std{z} &= \dfrac{\Norm{\DemeanedVec{z}}}{\sqrt{n}} \\[4pt]
            &= \dfrac{\Norm{z}}{\sqrt{n}} \qquad\qquad \because \, \Avg{z} = 0 \\[4pt]
            &= \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{n}} \left\lVert \dfrac{\sqrt{n}}{\Norm{\DemeanedVec{x}}} \ \,\DemeanedVec{x} \right\rVert \\[4pt]
            &= \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{n}} \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt{n}}{\Norm{\DemeanedVec{x}}} \cdot \Norm{\DemeanedVec{x}}
                \qquad\qquad \because \, \Norm{c\,\MVec{v}} = |c|\,\Norm{v} \emph{ for } c > 0 \\[4pt]
            &= 1
\end{align*}
